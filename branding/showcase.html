ImageUpscale

Eight directions for one idea: a low-resolution pixel becoming a higher-resolution one. Chosen: Quad Zoom.

Quad Zoom

One pixel becomes four: the exact thing a 2x upscale does. Three quiet quadrants at source resolution, one crisp subdivided quadrant as the single focal point. Asymmetric, mostly negative space.

SVG

Stair to Curve

A jagged pixel staircase on the left resolves into one smooth curve on the right: low resolution turning into detail. A single stroke, nothing else.

SVG

Resolution Ramp

A diagonal run of squares that shrink and multiply toward the top right: pixel density rising. Reads as motion and as a halftone at once.

SVG

Frame Grow

A small solid square inside a larger open frame whose corner is cut away toward the top right: the picture growing out of its own frame.

SVG

Aperture

A lens ring with a single sharp pixel at its centre. Focus and clarity; the centre square is the only hard edge in a round form.

SVG

Nested Up

Two rounded squares offset along the diagonal, the larger one carrying the smaller: 2x as a nesting. Solid over ghost for hierarchy.

SVG

Dot Matrix

A 3x3 dot grid where each dot is slightly larger toward the bottom left and the top right dot is ringed: a sampling grid being refined.

SVG

Split Diagonal

A square cut on the diagonal: the lower left half is a coarse 3x3 pixel block, the upper right half is solid and smooth. Before and after in one shape.
